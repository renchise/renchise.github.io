Website privacy

This page covers the static GitHub Pages website itself. App-specific privacy details live inside each app folder.

This website does not need account sign-in and does not intentionally collect personal data through its pages. If you add analytics, forms, or contact links later, update this page to match the final behavior.

What the site does

Simple informational pages with links to the two Android apps, their policies, and their release pages.

Data handling
