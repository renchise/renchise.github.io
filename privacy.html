Site privacy summary

This page explains the site-level privacy approach for the Renchise Apps website. App-specific privacy and security details live inside each app folder.

What this website does

The site is a static presentation hub. It does not require sign-in, and it is intended to show app information, release links, and policy pages.

Advertising disclosure

Where either app uses Google Mobile Ads or AdMob, that advertising should be disclosed in the app’s privacy page and support materials.

Local files and exports

When the apps store files locally, those files remain on the device unless the user exports, shares, backs up, or deletes them.

Placeholders

Closed-test links, production links, tester-group links, and support contacts are placeholders until you replace them with your own URLs.

For app-specific privacy, use the pages inside apps/vouchlink and apps/vaultstudio.
