Renchise VaultStudio privacy policy

Keep this page inside the app folder so wording stays specific to the app and easy to update.

What the app may process

  • The app may store scanned ID images, passport photos, exported files, and app settings locally on the device.
  • If the user exports, shares, or backs up a file, that transfer is controlled by the user’s chosen app or service.
  • The app may show ads through Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, which has its own policies and settings.
  • No account is required unless you later add one and disclose it here.

Data storage

The app is designed to keep local data on the device unless the user chooses to export, share, or back it up.

If you later add cloud sync, clearly describe the service, retention, and deletion path here.

Advertising

Ads in this app should be disclosed as Google Mobile Ads / AdMob advertising. Google handles ad-serving and related identifiers according to its own policies and the user’s device settings.

Deletion and retention

Content remains until the user deletes it, removes the app, clears app data, or exports it elsewhere.
