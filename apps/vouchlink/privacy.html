Renchise VouchLink Privacy

Renchise VouchLink respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data storage & sync

  • Local only: All vouchers, links, or bookmarks you save are stored exclusively on your device.
  • No syncing: The app does not upload or sync your data to any cloud service or other devices.
  • Sharing: When you use the system share sheet, data is passed directly to the target app. Renchise VouchLink does not collect or log that activity.

Your control & deletion

  • Full access: You can view and manage all saved entries at any time within the app.
  • Easy deletion: Use the edit or settings options to remove individual items or clear all data.
  • Uninstall clears everything: Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data from your device.

Ads & third‑party services

Renchise VouchLink may display ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) or similar services. These ad providers operate independently and their data collection is governed by their own privacy policies. They do not have access to the vouchers or links you store inside the app.

Policy updated: This privacy policy was last revised on 10 August 2026. We may update it from time to time; please check back periodically.
