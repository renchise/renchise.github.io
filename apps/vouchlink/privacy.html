VouchLink privacy

Describe what the app stores, where it stores data, whether links or vouchers are synced, and how users can delete saved items.

Typical points to include

  • Local-only or synced storage
  • Share-sheet behavior
  • AdMob disclosure
  • Deletion and backup controls
