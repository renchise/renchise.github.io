Core Features & Tools

Powerful tools for passport compliance, secure offline ID storage, and creative studio editing.

📷 Passport Photo Studio

Automated framing and compliance checks for international travel documents.

  • 🌐 50+ Country Standards – Pre-configured templates for passports, visas, and ID cards worldwide
  • ✨ Auto Background Replacement – Clean background cutout and replacement with standard white/light grey
  • 📏 Precision Framing & Crop – Automatic head sizing and eye alignment guidance
  • ⚠️ Guidance & Compliance Checks – Automated checks for lighting, face orientation, and shadow detection

🔐 Encrypted ID Vault

Private, offline storage for sensitive identity documents.

  • 📇 Front & Back Scans – Store structured front/back captures of driver's licenses, IDs, and membership cards
  • 🔒 100% Offline Storage – All scans remain strictly on your device; zero cloud upload
  • 📁 Organized Document Gallery – Quick search, categorization, and easy local viewing
  • 📤 User-Controlled Export – Share or print documents only when explicitly initiated by you

🎨 Studio Editing Tools

Versatile creative photo editing independent of passport requirements.

  • ✂️ Background Removal – Cut out subjects from photo backgrounds with 1 tap
  • 📐 Canvas Resizing & Crop – Custom aspect ratios and dimensions for digital or print use
  • ✍️ Text Overlays & Watermarks – Add custom labels, notes, or privacy watermarks
  • 🎛️ Fine Color Adjustments – Tweak brightness, contrast, and tone for crisp exports

🛡️ Device Lock & Security

Native security protections for sensitive app content.

  • 👆 Biometric / PIN App Lock – Protect app access using your device's native lock screen (Fingerprint, PIN, Pattern)
  • 🚫 Zero Cloud Analytics on Scans – Document scans and photos are never processed by external servers
  • 📱 On-Device AI Models – Background removal and face alignment run locally on your phone
