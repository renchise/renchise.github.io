About VaultStudio

Renchise VaultStudio combines passport photo preparation, an ID Vault for scanned documents, and a separate Studio workspace. It is designed as a practical personal utility, not as an official government, immigration, or identity platform.

  • Singapore-first passport photo workflow
  • Automatic background replacement or smart preserve mode
  • ID Vault for front/back scans and document storage
  • Optional device-security app lock on supported phones
  • Studio tools for simple background and canvas editing

Privacy

VaultStudio is designed to keep user-selected photos and scans under your control unless you choose to export or share them. Ads may appear via Google AdMob on supported screens.

Security

Use device lock where available. Keep sensitive scans private, and share only when you intend to send a copy outside the app.
