About VaultStudio

Renchise VaultStudio combines three independent tools in one app:

  • Passport Photo – Automatic framing, background replacement, and compliance checks for over 50 country formats. Results are guidance only; final acceptance depends on the receiving authority.
  • ID Vault – Private, on‑device storage for front/back scans of identity documents. All data stays on your phone unless you choose to export or share.
  • Studio – Background removal, canvas resizing, text overlays, and creative editing—independent of the passport workflow.
  • Device lock – Optional screen‑lock integration (fingerprint/PIN/pattern) to restrict app access on supported devices.

VaultStudio is designed as a practical personal utility, not as an official government, immigration, or identity platform.

Privacy

VaultStudio processes all photos and scans on‑device. No image data is sent to Renchise servers. Ads via Google AdMob may use advertising identifiers as governed by Google’s policies.

Security

Use your device’s native screen lock to protect the app. Sensitive scans remain local—share only when you intend to send a copy outside the app.
