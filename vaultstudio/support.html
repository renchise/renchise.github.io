Frequently asked questions

Does VaultStudio store my photos or scans on your servers?
No. All processing and storage are on‑device. We do not have access to your content.

Is the passport photo guaranteed to be accepted?
No. The app provides automated guidance and compliance checks, but final acceptance depends on the rules and review of the receiving authority. Always verify against the official requirements.

Can I use the ID Vault for official document submission?
The ID Vault is a personal storage and review tool. It is not a certified identity platform. Use it for reference only; do not rely on it for official submissions without independent verification.

How do I enable the app lock?
Go to Settings within the app and toggle the "App lock" option. Your device must have a screen lock (PIN, pattern, password, or biometric) configured.

What permissions does the app need and why?
Camera – to take new photos. Storage – to read images from your gallery and save exports. Advertising ID – for AdMob ads. No permission is used outside its stated purpose.

How do I delete my stored ID scans?
Open the ID Vault, tap the edit icon on a document, and select "Delete". You can also delete individual pages when viewing a document.

Does the app work offline?
Yes, the core passport photo processing, ID Vault, and Studio tools work entirely offline. Internet access is only required for displaying ads via AdMob and for in‑app purchases (if applicable).

Independent app statement: VaultStudio is an independent personal app. It is not affiliated with any government, embassy, passport office, or identity authority.
