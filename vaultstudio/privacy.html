Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026

Renchise VaultStudio is designed with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your control over it.

1. Data processing is on‑device

All photos, ID scans, and related images you choose to open in VaultStudio are processed entirely on your device. No image data, document text, or biometric information is ever transmitted to Renchise servers or any third‑party servers (except where you explicitly choose to share or export a file via Android’s sharing system).

2. What data does the app access?

  • Camera – used only when you take a new photo from within the app. The resulting photo is stored on your device and never sent elsewhere.
  • Storage – used to read images you select from your gallery and to save exported photos or scans to your device’s Pictures folder.
  • Advertising ID – the app uses Google AdMob to show ads on certain screens. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID to serve personalized or non‑personalized ads, as governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device’s settings.

3. What data is not collected?

  • No photos, scans, or exported files are sent to Renchise or any third party without your explicit action (e.g., tapping “Share” or “Save”).
  • No facial recognition data, biometric templates, or document numbers are extracted or stored outside the app’s local database.
  • No usage analytics, crash logs, or personal identifiers are collected by Renchise.

4. Data you choose to share

When you use the “Share” or “Export” functions, the file is handed off to Android’s standard sharing flow. The destination app or service you select handles that data according to its own privacy policy. Renchise has no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, the privacy practices of third‑party apps or services.

5. Data retention

All files you create or store in the ID Vault remain on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes the app’s local database and cached files, but does not automatically delete files you have saved to your device’s gallery or Downloads folder.

6. Children’s privacy

VaultStudio is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at renchise.dev@gmail.com.

Independent app statement: VaultStudio is an independent personal app. It is not affiliated with any government, embassy, passport office, or identity authority.
