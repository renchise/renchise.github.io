Privacy policy

This page explains the intended privacy handling for Renchise VaultStudio.

VaultStudio is designed to let you work with passport photos, ID scans, and related images in a way that keeps them under your control. User-selected files are expected to stay on the device unless you intentionally export, print, or share them.

The app may show Google AdMob ads on supported pages. Ad display can involve Google’s advertising identifiers and related ad-delivery data as governed by Google’s policies.

When you choose to send a file to another app, the resulting transfer is handled by the device’s sharing flow and by the recipient app or service you choose.

  • No third-party authority is represented by this app.
  • Photos, scans, and exports remain your responsibility.
  • Use the app only with content you are allowed to process or share.
  • Review the output before publishing, submitting, or printing it.
