About VouchLink

Renchise VouchLink (formerly Renchise SG Voucher Bookmark) is a personal utility for organising voucher‑style bookmarks and reference links. It helps you keep track of your Singapore CDC Vouchers, Climate Vouchers, and any other bookmark you want to access quickly — all stored locally on your device.

The app is not a voucher issuer, merchant, government platform, or official representative. It is a personal tool built to help you organise information you already have.

  • 📩 Import from SMS – scan for official RedeemSG links automatically
  • 🔐 Local AES‑256 encryption – all data stays on your device
  • 📊 Balance tracking – Heartland, Supermarket, Climate balances with expiry alerts
  • 💾 Encrypted backup & restore – share or import securely
  • 🔗 Bookmark any link – organise with custom categories
  • 🌐 Built‑in browser – open voucher pages inside the app
  • 📢 Ad‑supported – Google AdMob ads (removable)
  • 🕶️ Dark Mode – Material Design 3 interface

🔐 Privacy

No account required. No cloud sync. All data stays on your device. SMS permission is used only to find voucher links — other messages are ignored. AdMob may collect device information per Google's policy.

🛡️ Security

AES‑256 encryption for all stored data. No data ever leaves your device. Backups are encrypted with the same key. Use your device lock to protect access.
