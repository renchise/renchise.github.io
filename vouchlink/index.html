About VouchLink

Renchise VouchLink is the current name for the app previously shown as Renchise SG Voucher Bookmark. It is presented as a simple personal utility for organising voucher-style bookmarks and related reference links.

  • Bookmark-style organisation for saved voucher links
  • Simple access to trusted reference items
  • Ads disclosure for Google AdMob-supported pages
  • Personal, independent app identity
  • Clear privacy, security, and disclaimer pages
