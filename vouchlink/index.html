About VouchLink

Renchise VouchLink is an independent third-party application for organizing website links and managing personal CDC Voucher and Climate Voucher links.

The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, sponsored by, or operated by the Singapore Government, GovTech Singapore, CDC, the Climate Vouchers programme, RedeemSG, or any government agency or voucher issuer.

Link Organizer – Available Worldwide

  • 🔗 Save and organize website links in groups
  • 🌐 Open in browser – use your device's default browser
  • 📋 Manage links – copy, share, edit, or delete any saved link
  • 🔐 Secure storage – all data is stored locally and encrypted on your device

Voucher Management – For SG Users

  • 📩 Import from SMS – scan for supported voucher links automatically
  • ➕ Manual add – add a voucher link manually
  • 📊 Balance tracking – view balance and expiry from saved voucher pages
  • 🔄 Manual refresh – refresh selected vouchers to update info
  • 💾 Encrypted backup – share or import securely

Policies & legal

Each policy has a visible summary here and a dedicated page for the full wording.

🔐 Privacy

No account required. No cloud sync. All data stays on your device. SMS permission is used only to find voucher links — other messages are ignored. AdMob may collect device information per Google's policy.

🛡️ Security

AES‑256 encryption for all stored data. No data ever leaves your device. Backups are encrypted with the same key. Use your device lock to protect access.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Independent app. Not affiliated with any government agency, voucher issuer, or merchant. Provided "as is" — use at your own risk. No liability for misuse or data loss.
