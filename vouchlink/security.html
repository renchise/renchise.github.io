Security page

Security notes for saved links, reference items, and shared content.

VouchLink is designed to work as a simple organiser for your own saved items. Keep the app on a device protected by your native screen lock and only share entries with recipients you trust.

If you store referral or voucher information inside the app, treat it as personal data. Avoid exposing it publicly, and delete items you no longer need.

  • Use your device lock and passcode where available.
  • Double-check links before sending them to others.
  • Keep sensitive saved items private.
  • Remove anything that is no longer needed.
