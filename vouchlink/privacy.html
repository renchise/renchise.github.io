Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 June 2026

⚠️ Independent App — No Affiliation
Renchise VouchLink is a personal, independent utility developed by an individual Android developer. It is not affiliated with any government agency, voucher issuer, merchant, or platform operator.

Your privacy is important. Renchise VouchLink has been designed with a privacy‑first approach. Most application data is stored only on your device.

🔒 Personal Information

The application does NOT:

  • Create user accounts
  • Require registration
  • Sell personal information
  • Upload voucher information to our servers
  • Share your voucher data with third parties

📦 Information Stored Locally

The following information may be stored on your device:

  • Voucher links
  • Voucher balances
  • Voucher names
  • Expiry dates
  • Postal code (where applicable)
  • Application preferences

📩 SMS Permission

READ_SMS permission is optional. When granted, it is used solely to locate supported (CDC & Climate) voucher SMS messages for bulk import. SMS messages unrelated to supported vouchers are ignored. Permission may be revoked at any time from Android Settings.

🌐 Internet Access

Internet access is used for:

  • Opening official voucher websites (e.g. https://redeem.gov.sg/)
  • Refreshing voucher information
  • Loading web content from official websites
  • Displaying advertisements (where applicable)

📢 Advertising

This application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain diagnostic, advertising or device information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your voucher data.

📊 Analytics

The application itself does not collect analytics about your voucher usage. No crash reporting tools are integrated.

☁ Cloud Storage

No cloud backup service is provided. Your voucher information remains stored on your own device unless you choose to export a backup.

🗑 Delete Your Data

All locally stored information can be removed via:
Android Settings → Apps → Renchise VouchLink → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage

Effective Date: 28 June 2026
