Privacy policy

This page explains the intended privacy handling for Renchise VouchLink.

VouchLink is presented as a personal bookmark-style utility. The app is designed so that saved entries, reference links, and similar content remain under the user’s control unless the user explicitly exports or shares them.

The app may show Google AdMob ads on supported pages. As with other AdMob-enabled experiences, Google may process advertising identifiers and related delivery data in line with its own policies.

  • Saved voucher or bookmark entries are for personal use.
  • Sharing occurs only when you choose to send or export content.
  • No third-party platform is represented by the app.
  • Always verify the destination before sharing links or references.
