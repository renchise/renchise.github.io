Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

⚠️ IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
Renchise VouchLink is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, sponsored by, or operated by the Singapore Government, GovTech Singapore, CDC, the Climate Vouchers programme, RedeemSG, or any government agency or voucher issuer.

Your privacy is important. Renchise VouchLink is an independent application designed with a privacy‑first approach. All data is processed and stored locally on your device.

🔒 Personal Information

No account or registration is required to use VouchLink. The application does NOT:

  • Create user accounts or require registration
  • Sell personal information
  • Upload voucher information to our servers
  • Share your voucher data with third parties (except when explicitly initiated by the user)

📦 Information Stored Locally

The following information may be stored on your device:

  • Voucher links
  • Voucher balances
  • Voucher names
  • Expiry dates
  • Postal code (where applicable)
  • Application preferences

📩 SMS Import

READ_SMS permission is optional and is only required when using the Batch Import from SMS feature. SMS import is user-initiated. When selected, VouchLink checks messages within a look-back period (default 365 days) for links matching the configured voucher URL prefix (default https://voucher.redeem.gov.sg).

Matching links are used to add or update voucher entries. Other messages are ignored. You can revoke this permission at any time.

🌐 Internet Access

Internet access is used for:

  • Opening official voucher websites (e.g. https://redeem.gov.sg/)
  • Refreshing voucher information
  • Loading web content from official websites
  • Displaying advertisements (where applicable)

📢 Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google AdMob may process certain device, advertising, and related information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

VouchLink may provide complimentary ad-removal codes to selected testers or users at the developer's discretion. These codes are provided free of charge and are not for sale.

📊 Analytics

The application itself does not collect analytics about your voucher usage. No crash reporting tools are integrated.

🔗 Private Voucher Links & Sharing

Voucher links are personal, user-specific redemption links. Users should keep these private. Sharing is entirely user-initiated and should only be done with intended recipients (e.g., family members).

All data is stored locally. Voucher links and information are not uploaded to or stored on Renchise servers or any cloud storage service. The developer has no access to your locally stored data.

☁ Cloud Storage

Voucher data has no cloud synchronisation or cloud storage. Your information remains stored on your own device unless you choose to export a backup.

🗑 Delete Your Data

All locally stored information can be removed via:
Android Settings → Apps → Renchise VouchLink → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage
