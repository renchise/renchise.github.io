Frequently asked questions

Does Renchise Journey Jigsaw store my custom photos on your servers?
No. All image scaling, cropping, and puzzle generation are performed 100% locally on your device. Your custom photo files are saved in your phone's private app storage and are never uploaded to any cloud server.

How do I create a custom photo puzzle?
Go to the Jigsaw Gallery, tap the "Vault" tab, and select "Add & Edit New Photo". Choose any photo from your phone, pinch to zoom, rotate 90° if needed, align within the 4:3 frame, and tap "Save to Library". You can now select it anytime to play!

What are the 81 Ordeals and Companion Character Perks?
The 81 Ordeals represent classical Journey to the West story levels. Completing puzzles unlocks mythic codex entries and companion characters (such as Sun Wukong, Tang Sanzang, and Guanyin). Equipping active companions grants passive perks like +15% coin bonuses, cheaper hints, free peeks, or extra time.

What is the Mystery Blind Box Challenge?
In Mystery Blind Box Challenge mode, the puzzle picture is chosen at random and preview peeking is disabled. Successfully completing a Mystery Blind Challenge awards 2× bonus coins!

Does the app work offline?
Yes. The core jigsaw puzzle engine, custom photo library, 81 Ordeals codex, achievements, and level saves work completely offline without an internet connection. Internet access is only used for loading optional AdMob ads.

How do I unlock new Board Themes or Photo Slots?
You earn virtual coins by completing jigsaw puzzles, earning 3-star ratings, keeping combo streaks, and claiming daily login rewards. Coins can be used to unlock mythical board themes (like Flower Fruit Mountain or Dragon Palace) and purchase extra photo slots in your vault.

How do I delete custom photos or reset game progress?
To delete an individual custom photo, open the Vault tab in the Jigsaw Gallery and tap the trash bin icon on the photo card. To reset all game data, go to Android Settings → Apps → Renchise Journey Jigsaw → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
