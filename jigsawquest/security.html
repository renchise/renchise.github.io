Security

How Renchise Journey Jigsaw protects your photos and game data

{% include app_disclaimer.html %}

1. 100% On‑device storage & Room Database

All game save files, level progress, unlocked achievements, companion codex entries, and custom photo puzzles are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database (Room, jigsaw.db) and app-private storage (filesDir/saved_photos). The app does not use remote cloud servers or developer databases for your personal content.

2. Android Application Sandbox

Renchise Journey Jigsaw relies on Android's built-in application sandboxing architecture. Database records, preferences, and custom photo files stored within the app's private directory are strictly isolated from other installed applications on your device.

3. Secure In-App Purchases via Google Play Billing

In-app purchases (such as coin bundles or vault slot upgrades) are handled exclusively through Google Play's secure billing service (billing-ktx). Financial data and payment credentials are processed entirely by Google Play; no payment information is ever exposed to or stored by Renchise.

4. Local Image Processing

When creating a custom jigsaw puzzle, all image manipulation — including 90° rotation, scaling, pinch-to-zoom alignment, and 4:3 aspect ratio cropping — is executed strictly on-device using local Android Canvas and Bitmap APIs. No image data is sent to external cloud processing endpoints.

5. Minimal Permission Surface

The app only requests standard permissions strictly needed for gameplay: VIBRATE for haptic feedback, standard Android Photo Picker for custom image import, INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for Google AdMob ads and Google Play billing validation, and AD_ID for advertising services.

6. User Data Control & Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. Custom photo puzzles can be deleted individually from the Jigsaw Gallery at any time. Clearing the app's cache and storage in Android System Settings permanently wipes all local Room database records and stored custom photo files.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not upload, store, or transmit your photos or scans on any remote server.
  • We do not track your location, contacts, or browsing history.
  • We do not collect personal identify information, login credentials, or account data.
  • We do not run background telemetry or crash logging tools that harvest personal data.
