Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 (Version 1.0)

{% include app_disclaimer.html %}

Your privacy is important to us. Renchise Jigsaw Quest (Package: com.renchise.puzzle) is an independent application designed with a privacy‑first approach. All core gameplay features, level progress, custom photo cropping, and companion codex data are processed and stored locally on your device.

🔒 Personal Information & Data Collection

No account, registration, or login is required to use Renchise Jigsaw Quest. The application does NOT:

  • Create user accounts or request email address, name, or phone number
  • Collect bank account, credit card, or financial details (all in-game coins are purely virtual)
  • Track your GPS, fine location, or coarse location
  • Sell, rent, or trade your personal information
  • Upload your custom photos or jigsaw puzzle progress to external servers

📦 Information Stored Locally

All game data is stored strictly on your device in a local SQLite database (Android Room) and private app storage:

  • Level progress, stars earned, and best completion times per rank
  • Unlocked achievements (72 unique achievements)
  • Player profile nickname, avatar selection, and active companion character ID
  • Saved puzzle state (board slots, tray pieces, rotations) for auto-resuming unfinished games
  • Daily login streak count and last claim date
  • Custom photo library items (cropped bitmaps saved in private app storage: filesDir/saved_photos)
  • Application preferences (music/SFX volume, haptics, active board theme, contrast setting, font scale, language)

🖼️ Custom Photo Privacy

When you use the Add & Edit New Photo feature, you select an image from your device via the standard Android photo picker. The selected photo is rotated, scaled, and cropped into a 4:3 ratio strictly on-device using local Android graphic APIs.

Your custom photos are saved exclusively to your device's local app storage. They are never transmitted to our servers, uploaded to any cloud service, or accessible to third parties.

🛡️ System Permissions & Purpose

The app requests only minimal Android permissions necessary for gameplay features:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE – Optional haptic feedback during piece snapping and button interactions. Can be toggled off in game settings.
  • Photo / Media Access – User-initiated file picker for selecting a custom photo to turn into a jigsaw puzzle.
  • android.permission.INTERNET – Required for loading advertisements via Google AdMob and standard Google Play Store services.

📢 Advertising & Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP addresses, and advertising IDs in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob Terms.

Renchise Jigsaw Quest does not use any third-party behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs outside of standard AdMob ad delivery.

🗑️ Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information at all times:

  • Delete Individual Custom Photos – Open the Jigsaw Gallery in the app, switch to the "Vault" tab, tap the trash bin icon on any custom photo, and confirm deletion.
  • Delete All Game Data & Photos – Open Android Settings → Apps → Renchise Jigsaw Quest → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage. This permanently removes all local progress, coins, achievements, and custom photo puzzles.
  • Uninstalling the App – Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally stored database files and custom photo puzzles.

👶 Children's Privacy

Renchise Jigsaw Quest is a general audience family-friendly puzzle game. The app does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age).
