Core Features & Gameplay Capabilities

Everything you need for rich mythological story puzzles, custom photo jigsaw creation, and relaxing offline play.

📜 Journey to the West & 81 Ordeals Codex

Immerse yourself in classical Eastern mythology with rich lore and hero companion perks.

  • 📜 81 Mythological Ordeals – Explore 81 classical story puzzle levels with historical lore backgrounds
  • 🛡️ 18 Companion Characters – Unlock Sun Wukong, Tang Sanzang, Guanyin, Bull Demon King, and more with unique passive perks
  • 📖 Journey Codex Collection – Track unlocked characters, avatars, and lore entries as you progress
  • ✨ Passive Boosts – Earn coin multipliers, hint discounts, extra time, and free peeks from active companions

📸 Custom Photo Jigsaw Library

Turn your personal memories and photos into interactive jigsaw puzzles.

  • 📸 Photo Gallery Import – Select any image from your device gallery to build a puzzle
  • ✂️ Built-in Rotate & Crop Editor – Pinch to zoom, rotate 90°, and crop to optimal 4:3 puzzle ratio
  • 🖼️ Private Photo Vault – Store custom photo puzzles in your personal puzzle library
  • 🔒 100% Private & On-Device – Custom photos are processed locally and never uploaded to any cloud server

🎮 10 Difficulty Ranks & Gameplay Modes

From casual relaxing puzzles to expert 300-piece challenges.

  • 🧩 10 Journey Ranks – Scalable piece counts from 12-piece (Little Demon) up to 300-piece (Tathagata)
  • 🧘 Zen Mode – Play without time limits for pure relaxation
  • 🔄 Piece Rotation Toggle – Add 4-way piece rotation for enhanced challenge
  • 🎲 Mystery Blind Box Challenge – Test your skills with hidden preview images for 2× coin rewards
  • ⚡ Smart Tray Tools – Filter border vs inner pieces, auto-sort tray, and use Golden Talisman auto-snaps

🏆 72 Achievements, Themes & Streaks

Comprehensive offline progression, daily rewards, and visual themes.

  • 🏆 72 Unique Achievements – Unlock achievements for speed, combos, star milestones, and wealth
  • 🎨 6 Mythological Board Themes – Customize your game with Lingxiao Hall, Flower Fruit Mountain, Water Curtain Cave, Flame Mountain, Dragon Palace, and Moon Palace
  • 🎁 Daily Login Streak Rewards – Claim daily login bonuses with escalating coin rewards
  • 🔍 High-Contrast & Sound Options – Toggle high-contrast board slots, haptic vibration, and music volume
