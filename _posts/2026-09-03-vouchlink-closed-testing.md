Following the announcement of VaultStudio, we are also opening up **VouchLink** for closed testing! This represents the second of the first two apps developed by Renchise.

### What is VouchLink?
VouchLink is a simple, private utility for organizing your everyday bookmarks and specialized voucher links (like SG CDC and Climate Vouchers). It focuses on local encryption to keep your personal links organized and secure.

### Become a Beta Tester
Building high-quality, reliable tools is my top priority. By joining the closed testing program, you’ll be among the first to use VouchLink and provide direct feedback that will shape the final version of the app.

### How to Join:
1. Join our [Google Tester Group](https://groups.google.com/g/renchise_closed_testers).
2. Opt-in via the [Web Link](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.renchise.sgvoucher).
3. Download the app on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.renchise.sgvoucher).

We appreciate your time and effort in helping an independent developer bring these projects to life!
