We are thrilled to announce that **VaultStudio**, one of the first two apps from Renchise, is now officially in the **Closed Testing** phase on the Google Play Store!

### What is VaultStudio?
VaultStudio is an AI-powered passport photo tool and secure ID scanner. It’s designed to help you create compliant photos for travel and store your sensitive documents safely on your device.

### We Need Your Help!
As an independent developer launching my first apps, your feedback is invaluable. We are looking for testers to join our closed group to help us identify bugs, improve the user interface, and ensure the app is ready for a full production release.

### How to Join:
1. Join our [Google Tester Group](https://groups.google.com/g/renchise_closed_testers).
2. Opt-in via the [Web Link](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.renchise.passportphoto).
3. Download the app on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.renchise.passportphoto).

Thank you for supporting independent development and helping us make VaultStudio the best it can be!
