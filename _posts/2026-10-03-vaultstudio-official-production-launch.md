We are thrilled to announce that **Renchise VaultStudio** is officially **LIVE on the Google Play Store worldwide**!

Following a successful closed testing phase with our community, VaultStudio is now publicly available for Android users globally.

### What is Renchise VaultStudio?
VaultStudio combines three powerful, on-device tools in one private app:
- 📷 **AI Passport Photo Studio:** Automatic framing, background removal, and compliance checks for over 50 international passport and visa photo standards.
- 🔐 **Secure ID Vault:** Private, on-device storage for front and back scans of identity documents, protected with optional device screen lock (fingerprint, PIN, pattern).
- 🎨 **Creative Studio Tools:** Background replacement, canvas resizing, text overlays, and photo adjustments.

### 🌐 100% On-Device Privacy
VaultStudio processes all photos, scans, and documents **entirely on your phone**. No image data, biometric info, or identity documents are ever uploaded to remote servers.
