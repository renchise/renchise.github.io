We are thrilled to announce that **Renchise Kaki** is officially **LIVE on the Google Play Store** as of **25 October 2026**!

Following VaultStudio's recent release, Renchise Kaki brings a simple, private, all-in-one utility tailored for Singapore residents to manage CDC and Climate Vouchers, track daily local expenses, split group bills, and organize website links.

---

### 🎟️ What is Renchise Kaki?

Renchise Kaki is designed as an everyday digital companion for Singapore residents:

- 🎟️ **SG CDC & Climate Voucher Manager:** Automatically scan and import RedeemSG voucher links (`https://voucher.redeem.gov.sg`) directly from SMS inbox with auto-deduplication. Enjoy 1-tap balance refreshes and keep voucher links private.
- ☕ **Quick Expense Tracker (Local SGD):** Easily log daily spending, view trend charts by day/week/month/year, and organize custom categories.
- 🧾 **Group Bill Split:** Effortlessly split dining or outing bills for up to 50 people across Equal, Individual, and Custom split modes, attach receipt photos, and share itemized summaries via WhatsApp or messaging.
- 🔑 **Family Code & CSV Backup:** Safely share CDC & Climate vouchers with family members using a 120-second expiring QR code, or export general website bookmark CSV backups.

---

### 🔒 100% On-Device & Private

Renchise Kaki requires **no account sign-ups, no cloud sync, and no bank credential access**. All expense records, voucher bookmarks, and bill split history stay **100% private on your device**.
