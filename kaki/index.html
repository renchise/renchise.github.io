About Renchise Kaki

Renchise Kaki is an independent app for Singapore residents. It helps you manage CDC and Climate Vouchers, track daily expenses, split dining bills, and organise website links — all in one private place.

The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, sponsored by, or operated by the Singapore Government, GovTech Singapore, CDC, the Climate Vouchers programme, RedeemSG, or any government agency or voucher issuer.

🎟️ Vouchers & Links

  • 📩 Auto-Import from SMS – scan inbox for official RedeemSG voucher links (https://voucher.redeem.gov.sg) with auto-de-duplication
  • ➕ Manual & CSV/HTML Import – add manually or batch-import bookmark files with preview
  • 🛒 CDC & Climate Tabs – Heartland, Supermarket, and NEA Climate Vouchers organized separately
  • 🔄 1-Tap Balance Refresh – update single voucher balances on demand or bulk-refresh via SMS scan
  • 🔒 Private Redemption Links – keep voucher links private; sharing is strictly user-initiated

☕ Quick Expenses (Local SGD)

  • ➕ Log Spending – record amount in SGD, date, category type, and description
  • 📅 Date Filters & Charts – filter by Today, Week, Month, or Year with daily/weekly/monthly trend charts
  • 🏷️ Custom Categories – add, rename, or soft-archive custom expense types
  • 🔐 100% On-Device – no bank details or account logins required; zero cloud tracking

🧾 Bill Split (Group Meals & Expenses)

  • 👥 Up to 50 People – manage dining and group outing bills with friends and family
  • 🔀 3 Split Modes – Equal, Individual (specific items), and Custom (own items + shared treats)
  • 📷 Bill Photo Capture – snap physical receipt photos with auto-crop framing
  • 🔍 Flexible Assignment – assign items to Everyone or "Everyone except X"
  • 📤 Itemised Sharing – share totals and attached bill photos via WhatsApp, Email, or messaging
  • 💾 Local History & Payment Status – track who has paid with built-in checkboxes

🔑 Family Code & 📁 CSV Backup

  • 🔑 Family Voucher Sharing – share CDC and Climate vouchers using a private Family Code via 120-second expiring QR codes or text payload
  • 📁 General Link CSV Backup – export, import, or share general web link CSV backups (government CDC/Climate vouchers strictly excluded for security)

Policies & legal

Each policy has a visible summary here and a dedicated page for the full wording.

🔐 Privacy

No account required. No cloud sync. All data (vouchers, expenses, bill splits, Family Code) stays on your device. SMS permission is optional. AdMob may process device info per Google's policy.

🛡️ Security

On-device encryption for stored data. Zero cloud servers. Expiring 120s QR code for Family Code sharing. Government vouchers excluded from CSV backups.

Independent app. Not affiliated with any government agency or voucher issuer. Provided "as is". No liability for misuse or data loss.
