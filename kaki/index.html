All-in-One Personal Utility for Singapore

Renchise Kaki helps you manage CDC and Climate Vouchers, track daily local expenses in SGD, split group dining bills, and organize web links — all in one private, on-device app.

Core Features & Capabilities

Everything you need for everyday voucher management, spending tracking, and group expense splitting.

🎟️ Vouchers & Link Organizer

Organize official Singapore CDC & Climate vouchers alongside your general web links.

  • 📩 Auto-Import from SMS – Scan inbox for official RedeemSG voucher links (https://voucher.redeem.gov.sg) with automatic deduplication
  • 🛒 CDC & Climate Tabs – Separate tabs for Heartland, Supermarket, and NEA Climate Vouchers
  • 🔄 1-Tap Balance Refresh – Update individual voucher balances on demand or bulk-refresh via SMS scan
  • ➕ Manual & CSV Import – Easily add links manually or batch-import bookmark files
  • 🔒 Private Redemption Links – Voucher links remain local; sharing is strictly user-initiated

☕ Quick Expense Tracker (Local SGD)

Fast, zero-friction daily expense logging designed for SGD spending.

  • ➕ Log Daily Spending – Record amount in SGD, date, category, and description in seconds
  • 📅 Date Filters & Charts – View daily/weekly/monthly trends with filter presets (Today, Week, Month, Year)
  • 🏷️ Custom Categories – Add, rename, or soft-archive custom expense types to match your routine
  • 🔐 100% On-Device Privacy – Zero bank credentials, no account login, and zero cloud tracking

🧾 Group Bill Split

Seamless group meal and outing bill splitting with friends and family.

  • 👥 Up to 50 People – Manage large group dining and trip expenses with ease
  • 🔀 3 Split Modes – Equal, Individual (per item), and Custom (own items + shared treats)
  • 📷 Bill Photo Capture – Snap receipt photos with auto-crop framing
  • 📤 Itemised Sharing – Share totals and attached bill photos via WhatsApp, Email, or messaging
  • 💾 Local Payment Tracking – Mark who has paid with built-in checkboxes

🔑 Family Code & 📁 CSV Backup

Secure family sharing and data export features.

  • 🔑 Family Voucher Sharing – Share CDC & Climate vouchers with family using a private Family Code via 120-second expiring QR code
  • 📁 General Link CSV Backup – Export, import, or share general web link CSV backups
  • 🛡️ Voucher Security Rule – Official government CDC/Climate vouchers are strictly excluded from CSV exports for your protection
