Security Practices

How your data is protected in Renchise Kaki (Version 1.11).

⚠️ IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER

Renchise Kaki is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, sponsored by, or operated by the Singapore Government, GovTech Singapore, CDC, the Climate Vouchers programme, RedeemSG, or any government agency or voucher issuer.

🔐 Local Storage & Private Processing
All Renchise Kaki data (Vouchers, Quick Expenses, Bill Split records, and Family Code) is processed and stored locally on your device. Information is not uploaded to or stored on Renchise servers.

Encryption

All app data is encrypted using AES‑256 encryption before being saved to the local Room database on your device. Key derivation uses SHA‑256 passphrase digests to ensure robust protection against unauthorized local inspection.

Local Storage Only

All saved voucher links, balances, expense records, bill split details, receipt photos, and preferences stay exclusively in local SQLite database storage on your device. There is no cloud synchronisation and no remote database.

Family Code Sharing Security

When sharing CDC or Climate vouchers with family members using the Family Code feature, QR code payloads are generated with a strict 120-second time-to-live (TTL) expiry. This prevents old or discarded QR code screenshots from being misused.

CSV Backup Security Rules

The General Links CSV Backup feature allows users to export and import general web links. For safety and security, government CDC and Climate vouchers are strictly excluded from CSV backup files.

Permissions

  • READ_SMS (optional) — required only for user-initiated SMS batch import of official RedeemSG voucher links.
  • CAMERA (optional) — required only when capturing physical receipt photos for Bill Split.
  • INTERNET — used to open official voucher web pages and display AdMob ads.

Security Recommendations

  • Enable device lock (PIN / pattern / biometric) to secure your device.
  • Keep your device OS updated.
  • Do not share Family Code QR code screenshots publicly.
  • Only share voucher links with trusted family members or friends.

Effective Date: October 2026 (Version 1.11)
