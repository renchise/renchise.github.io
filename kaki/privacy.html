Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 (Version 1.11)

{% include app_disclaimer.html %}

Your privacy is important. Renchise Kaki is an independent application designed with a privacy‑first approach. All data is processed and stored locally on your device.

🔒 Personal Information

No account or registration is required to use Renchise Kaki. The application does NOT:

  • Create user accounts or require registration
  • Collect bank account, credit card, or payment details
  • Sell personal information
  • Upload voucher, expense, or bill split data to our servers
  • Share your data with third parties (except when explicitly initiated by the user, e.g., sharing a bill split summary or family QR payload)

📦 Information Stored Locally

All app data is stored strictly on your device in a local encrypted database (Room SQLite):

  • Saved links and voucher links
  • Voucher balances, names, and expiry dates
  • Quick Expense records (amount in SGD, date, type, description)
  • Bill Split records (participant names, item assignments, bill photos, payment statuses)
  • Family Code settings
  • Application preferences and reminder configurations

📩 SMS Import

READ_SMS permission is optional and is only required when using the Batch Import from SMS feature. SMS import is user-initiated. When selected, Renchise Kaki checks messages within a look-back period (default 365 days) for links matching the configured voucher URL prefix (default https://voucher.redeem.gov.sg).

Matching links are used to add or update voucher entries. Other messages are ignored. You can revoke this permission at any time in Android system settings.

🌐 Internet Access

Internet access is used for:

  • Opening official voucher websites (e.g. https://redeem.gov.sg/)
  • Refreshing voucher information on demand
  • Loading web content from official websites
  • Displaying advertisements (where applicable)

📢 Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google AdMob may process certain device, advertising, and related information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Renchise Kaki may provide complimentary ad-removal codes to selected testers or users at the developer's discretion. These codes are provided free of charge and are never sold.

📊 Analytics

The application itself does not collect analytics about your voucher, expense, or bill split usage. No crash reporting tools are integrated.

🔗 Private Voucher Links & Sharing

Voucher links are personal redemption links. Users should keep these private. Sharing (e.g., via Family Code QR code or messaging) is entirely user-initiated and should only be done with trusted recipients.

☁ Cloud Storage

All features (Vouchers, Quick Expenses, Bill Split, Link Organizer) operate with zero cloud synchronisation or cloud storage. Your data remains on your own device.

🗑 Delete Your Data

All locally stored information can be removed at any time via:
Android Settings → Apps → Renchise Kaki → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage

Effective Date: October 2026 (Version 1.11)
